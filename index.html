When is the best time to climb Kilimanjaro?
January–March and June–October offer the best weather conditions with clearer skies. We do climb year-round with proper equipment and preparation — our guides are experienced in all conditions.
When does the Great Migration happen?
June–July in the Serengeti western corridor, and August–November near the Masai Mara border. We track the herds in real time and adjust itineraries to maximize your sightings.
Do I need a visa for Tanzania?
Most nationalities can get a visa on arrival for $50–100. We provide invitation letters if required, and can advise on the e-visa application process ahead of travel.
How fit do I need to be for Kilimanjaro?
Moderate fitness is required — no technical climbing skills needed. We recommend starting hikes 3–4 months before. Our guides adjust pace to maximize success, which is why we achieve 98%.
What's included in the package prices?
Park fees, all meals during the trip, accommodation, professional guides, porter wages, and transfers. International flights are not included. Zero hidden fees — we are fully transparent.
How do I book and what are the payment terms?
Contact us via WhatsApp, email, or the form below. We require a 30% deposit to confirm your spot, with the balance due 30 days before arrival. Bank transfers and card payments accepted.
