Frequently Asked

🏔️ Best time to climb Kilimanjaro?

January–March and June–October offer the best weather. We climb year-round with proper gear.

🦁 When is the Great Migration?

June–July in Serengeti western corridor, and August–November in Masai Mara border. We track it live.

✈️ Do I need a visa?

Most nationalities get visa on arrival ($50-100). We provide invitation letters if needed.

💪 How fit for Kili?

Moderate fitness required. We recommend hiking beforehand. Our guides adjust pace for success.

💰 What’s included in prices?

Park fees, meals, accommodation, guides, porter wages. International flights extra. No hidden fees.

📞 How to book?

Contact us via WhatsApp, email, or form. Pay 30% deposit, balance before arrival. Secure.
