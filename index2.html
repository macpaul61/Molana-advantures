Our story

A Locally Owned Tanzanian Adventure Company

2012Founded in
Dar es Salaam

Molana Adventures is a 100% locally owned Tanzanian tour operator, founded in Dar es Salaam in 2012 by passionate guides who grew up exploring Tanzania's extraordinary wilderness.

We are built on a simple belief: the best safaris are led by people who know the land, love the animals, and care deeply about the communities that share this wilderness.

Our team of professionally certified guides brings decades of experience across the Serengeti, Kilimanjaro, Zanzibar, and beyond. Every itinerary is tailor-made — we don't do one-size-fits-all.

We are committed to responsible tourism, supporting local communities, conservation efforts, and ensuring every traveler leaves Tanzania with memories — and a lasting respect for its wild beauty.
